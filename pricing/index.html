Questions answered

Online training FAQs

How much does online personal training cost?

Monthly packages are ₹9,999 for 3 days a week, ₹11,999 for 4 days a week, and ₹14,999 for 5 days a week. International prices are $149, $199, and $249 respectively.

How many sessions are included?

The 3-day package includes 12 sessions, the 4-day package includes 16 sessions, and the 5-day package includes 20 sessions. Each package is valid for 1 month.

Can I train from home?

Yes. Online sessions can be planned around the space and equipment available at home, or around a gym setup if that is where you train.

Is nutrition guidance included?

Customized diet and nutrition guidance is listed as an inclusion in the online personal training packages. A diet-only package is also available without exercise sessions.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Online coaching is available for clients in India and internationally. International package prices are shown in USD on this page.

How do I choose the right package?

Start with the number of live sessions that fits your week. Sandhya can discuss your goals, schedule, fitness level, and training space before you decide.

Do I need gym equipment?

Not necessarily. The online service can be adapted to your available space and equipment. Ask about your specific setup before starting.

How can I get started?

Use any package enquiry button on this page to contact Sandhya on WhatsApp and discuss the right next step.
