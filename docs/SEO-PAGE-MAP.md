## Important architectural notes

- The site is a static HTML site with no framework or build pipeline.
- The canonical domain should remain the non-www domain to avoid duplicate host signals.
- Legacy `www` URLs and older route slugs should be consolidated to the canonical production URLs.
- Service landing pages are the primary commercial pages; blog pages should support those pages with topical authority and natural internal links.
- The page inventory does not show any separate legal pages, author pages, or category pages; those may be future additions and should not be created unless there is a real business need.
